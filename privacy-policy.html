Privacy Policy

Welcome to Durgesh Bookmark. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

Durgesh Bookmark is primarily a collection of useful websites and online resources. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information from visitors through the main bookmark pages.

2. Information Submitted Through Contact Form

If you contact us through our contact form, you may provide information such as your name, email address, subject and message.

This information is used only for responding to your message, handling suggestions, reporting broken links, or addressing website-related questions.

3. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, browser information, device information and general website usage data.

Google Analytics operates according to Google's own policies and terms.

4. Microsoft Clarity

We may use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with our website and to improve usability.

Microsoft Clarity may collect information about website interactions according to Microsoft's privacy practices.

5. Cookies

Our website and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, functionality and advertising purposes.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features.

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled on this website, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to display and measure advertisements.

Advertising and consent practices may vary depending on the visitor's location and applicable requirements.

7. External Links

Durgesh Bookmark contains links to third-party websites. When you click an external link, you leave our website and visit another website.

Third-party websites have their own privacy policies, terms and practices. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to maintain the security of our website. However, no online service can guarantee complete security of information.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from children through this website.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, analytics services, advertising services, or other practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the website, please visit our Contact Us page.
